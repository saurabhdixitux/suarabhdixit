About

Research-first.
Systems-minded.

I'm a Senior Product Designer and UX Research Lead with 10+ years in enterprise UX. Currently working through Infogain with clients including Riverbed Technology, HP Inc., and Samsung.

My work spans the full UX lifecycle — from ethnographic fieldwork and affinity mapping to shipping production-ready Figma component libraries and WCAG 2.1 AA documentation. I run dual-track Agile alongside engineering and product teams, ensuring discovery never falls behind delivery.

I hold certifications in GenAI and Prompt Engineering and completed the UX Quality Improvement Programme at IIT Bombay.
